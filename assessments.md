### Why the Holland Code matters most here

**AIR — Artistic, Investigative, Social** is the most directly actionable result in this file,
because Holland maps to work content rather than temperament:

- **Artistic** — product storytelling, visual merchandising, brand and craft
- **Investigative** — buying, analysis, product knowledge, pattern-finding
- **Social** — training, client development, teaching, enablement

Roles that hit all three should be weighted highest. Note that **Hertex Product Knowledge
Coordinator — training sales consultants on product — is close to a perfect AIR match**, and is
also the most advanced application in the pipeline. That is not certain to be causal, but it is
worth noticing.

Roles that hit only one, or that demand Enterprising-dominant behaviour (aggressive closing,
self-promotion, commission-only), sit against the profile — which is consistent with both the
anti-archetype list and the DiSC Steadiness result.

## How this steers the search

**Weight roles UP when they involve:** judgement under ambiguity · consultative long-horizon
relationships · pattern-finding across domains · deep domain knowledge as the moat · autonomy ·
number-two-to-founder scope.

**Weight roles DOWN when they require:** financial modelling as the core of the job · PMO-style
process discipline without an operations partner · personal-brand or thought-leadership output ·
aggressive stretch-target cultures · high-visibility low-autonomy environments.

**Level:** senior IC / manager / head-of-function in a small business. Sell depth of domain and
span of judgement, not people-management scale.
