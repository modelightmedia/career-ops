## Offer of employment

It gives me great pleasure to offer you the position of Sales Account Executive at OrbitX. The terms and conditions of your employment are set out below.

### 1. Position and reporting line

You will be employed as **Sales Account Executive**, reporting directly to Frans Rossouw, CEO.

The agreed segmentation is that you will focus on this premium segment, while existing accounts in the agricultural sector, including farms and vineyards, will continue to be serviced by the rest of the sales team.

### 2. Duties and responsibilities

Your core duties include:

- Cold calling and networking to develop new business
- Conducting client meetings across target industries
- Performing on-site assessments and lighting measurements
- Preparing and presenting tailored lighting solutions to clients
- Building and managing long-term client relationships
- Meeting agreed sales targets
- Maintaining accurate and current CRM records
- Representing OrbitX at industry events and on client sites

### 3. Performance indicators

Performance will be reviewed against both leading and lagging indicators, on a directional basis in Year 1 to allow for ramp into the role:

- **Leading indicators:** pipeline value, new accounts engaged, meetings held, on-site assessments completed, CRM hygiene
- **Lagging indicators:** new business closed, total portfolio value, conversion rate from quote to close

Year 1 is recognized as a ramp year. Performance trajectory will be reviewed at the 3-month and 6-month checkpoints (see clause 11) and quarterly thereafter.

### 4. Place and hours of work

The place of work is the OrbitX offices at 13 Suid Street, Paarl, and on the road visiting customers.

### 5. Fixed remuneration

OrbitX offers you a fixed monthly salary of **R25,000 before tax and statutory deductions**.

### 6. Commission

Commission is paid in addition to fixed salary, calculated as follows:

- X = OrbitX sales from existing customers serviced by the rest of the sales team
- Y = Sales from new customers landed by Jason

The commission percentage tier is determined by the sum of X and Y for the month:

| Total monthly portfolio (X + Y) | Commission tier |
|---|---|
| Less than R1 million | 5% |
| R1 million to less than R2 million | 6% |
| R2 million or greater | 7% |

**Commission paid = commission percentage × Y.**

The 6% and 7% tiers are subject to discounting on Jason's deals. Where a discount is provided to close a deal, the applicable commission percentage on that deal may revert to 5%.

**Worked examples:**

- *Example A:* The rest of the sales team's existing customers pay R800,000 (X). Jason's new customers pay R400,000 (Y). X + Y = R1.2m, qualifying for the 6% tier. Commission = 6% × R400,000 = R24,000.
- *Example B:* The rest of the sales team's existing customers pay R500,000 (X). Jason's new customers pay R300,000 (Y). X + Y = R800,000, qualifying for the 5% tier. Commission = 5% × R300,000 = R15,000.

Commission is payable at the end of the month for sales that were fully paid by the customer by the 20th of that month.

### 7. Future remuneration changes

Should OrbitX elect to restructure remuneration at any future date, including a transition to a fully commission-based model, the minimum fixed monthly remuneration shall not fall below R25,000, exclusive of travel reimbursement.

This structure is reimbursive rather than a fixed allowance and is therefore not subject to PAYE on the portion that does not exceed the prescribed rate, in line with current SARS regulations.

The starting reimbursement budget is R5,000 per month, reviewable at any time should this prove inadequate to cover actual business travel.
